PROGRAMS

Not sure which one fits? At the trial session a coach suggests a program for your goal.

Gym

Machines and free weights, with a coach on the floor to fix your form when you need it.

Personal training

Losing weight, getting stronger or coming back after a break? Your coach builds a plan for that goal and works with you every session.

Kickboxing

Classes for beginners and advanced. You learn technique on the bag and pads, and you spar only when your coach says you are ready.

Group conditioning

Coach-led group sessions for strength and endurance. You keep the pace, the group keeps you going.

HOW TO START

01

Send a message

Leave your number in the form or text us on WhatsApp. We get back to you to set a time.

02

Come to a trial session

See the gym, meet the coaches, and we check your level and your goal.

03

Train with a plan

Pick a membership or package. You continue the plan from your trial session on day one.
