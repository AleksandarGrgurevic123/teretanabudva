OUR APPROACH: START WITH A COACH

IT’S NOT WILLPOWER. IT’S STARTING WITHOUT A PLAN

A membership gets you into the gym, not a plan. Programs from the internet are not made for your level. That’s why everyone here starts in three steps.

01

Assessment

At the trial session the coach sees how you move, what hurts and what you want.

02

Plan

You get a plan for your first weeks: which exercises, how often and in what order.

03

Correction

In your sessions the coach corrects your form and adjusts the plan as you progress.

QUESTIONS BEFORE YOUR FIRST VISIT

Do I need to be in shape to start?

No. Most people come to the trial session out of shape. The coach adjusts the exercises to your level, not the other way around.

Is the trial session free?

Yes. It takes about an hour: the coach sees how you move, you ask what you want to know and you get a plan for your first weeks. You don’t have to buy a membership.

Can I do kickboxing without sparring?

Yes. Sparring is optional. You work on technique, conditioning and the bag without it.

When is the gym least busy?

Mornings until 10:00 and after 21:00. If crowds bother you, the coach suggests times that work.

What should I bring to my first session?

Indoor trainers, a towel and a bottle of water. Lockers and showers are on site.
